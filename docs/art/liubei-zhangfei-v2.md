# Liu Bei and Zhang Fei sprite studies v2

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-14 to match Guan Yu v2. Each character has six tier-one front/back idle, move and attack poses. Liu Bei retains his horse-drawn chariot and twin swords with ivory/crimson robes. Zhang Fei retains his mount and serpent spear with dark armor and an ochre scarf. Display height, class and combat rules are unchanged. Higher promotion tiers are unchanged.

Sources, exact prompts, reproducible crop scripts, exported lossless WebPs, composited QA images and original backups live in `.studio/map-art/liubei-v2/` and `.studio/map-art/zhangfei-v2/`. Active files use the existing sprite directories and pose names. Source atlases are 1536-by-1024 RGB; painted checkerboards were removed with the existing pipeline, including enclosed lower-leg and wheel background islands. Twelve composited poses were inspected for silhouettes and slicing errors.

Verified all three brothers together in the Zhuojun battle at default stage camera zoom 1.6. No source code, gameplay data or remote assets changed in this pass.
